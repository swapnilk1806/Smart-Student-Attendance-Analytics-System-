How attendance is calculated

Attendance % = (Present credits + Late credits × {{ late_credit }}) ÷ Total recorded sessions × 100.
Present = 1.0 credit · Late = {{ late_credit }} credit (configurable in Settings) · Absent = 0 credits. Days with no recorded attendance are excluded from the denominator.
