Calculation Rule

Attendance % = (Present × 1.0 + Late × {{ late_credit }}) ÷ Recorded sessions × 100.
Sessions are only counted where attendance was actually recorded — unrecorded days are excluded from the denominator. Late credit is configurable in Settings.
